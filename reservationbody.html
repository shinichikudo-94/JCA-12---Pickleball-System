Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a facility?

Open “Request Reservation,” fill in all required fields, choose the facility and schedule, describe your activity, and press “Submit Request.”

Does submitting a request mean it is approved?

No. A submitted request is only a request. The authorized school administrator must review and approve it.

How can I check availability?

Open “Availability” and filter by facility or date. This prototype only shows requests stored in the current browser.

What if my requested time overlaps another request?

The prototype checks for overlapping requests saved on this device and warns you. Please confirm the final schedule with the school administrator.

Can I cancel or edit my request?

This demonstration version does not yet include user accounts or a cancellation/editing workflow. Contact the authorized administrator for changes.

Who should I contact if I need help?

Please contact the school office or the staff member responsible for facility scheduling. Add the official contact details here once they are confirmed.

About Us

About the Project

The JCSGO Facility Reservation System is a student project proposal intended to help organize requests for the Sky Garden and Skyline of JCSGO Christian Academy. It aims to make facility schedules easier to check, record reservation details in one place, and help reduce scheduling conflicts.

Project Objectives

  • Provide a convenient way to submit reservation requests.
  • Display saved requests and their current status.
  • Help prevent overlapping reservation times.
  • Support clearer communication between requesters and facility administrators.

Project Team
